<b><font size="5">Network v0.6: Artifacts</font></b>
<br>
<br>
<small>August 2026</small>
<br>
<br>

This version adds Artifacts, a place to publish tools, works and digital releases, each with its own title, description, cover image and page. Journeys get a public browsing page ("Explore and host curated experiences"), and the Journal gets its own header: "Your private log of thoughts and fragments".

<b>Key features</b>
<br>

- Host an Artifact: title, description, cover photo, its own URL slug
- Public artifact detail pages
- Public Journeys listing page
- Journal view with its own header
