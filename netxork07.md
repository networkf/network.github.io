<b><font size="5">Network v0.7: Artifact Marketplace</font></b>
<br>
<br>
<small>September 2026</small>
<br>
<br>

Artifacts become a full catalogue. Each one now has a category (Journey, Meditation, Mindspace or Protocol), a tagline, a price, a link, and a gallery of up to three photos that open in a lightbox. There is a public Artifacts browsing page, a My Artifacts page for your public, private and draft items, direct image upload, and a site-wide "Search Network" field.

<b>Key features</b>
<br>

- Public Artifacts browsing page
- Artifact categories: Journey, Meditation, Mindspace, Protocol
- Tagline, price and link on each artifact
- Gallery of up to three photos with a lightbox
- My Artifacts page (public, private, drafts)
- Direct image upload for artifact images
- "Search Network" search field
