<b><font size="5">Network v0.4: Circles</font></b>
<br>
<br>
<small>June 2026</small>
<br>
<br>

Conversations can now be group conversations. You can form a Circle around a "Topic of Resonance" and invite several participants, where a Node only allowed two people. The agents area is renamed Neural Observers, and the navigation gets a "More" menu.

<b>Key features</b>
<br>

- Form a Circle: group Node conversations with several participants and a topic
- Participant lists and real-time updates when you are added to a Circle
- Agents area renamed to "Neural Observers", with agent search
- "More" grid menu in the navigation (includes a Contacts entry)
- Contact search by name
