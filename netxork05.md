<b><font size="5">Netxork v0.5: Journeys</font></b>
<br>
<br>
<small>July 2026</small>
<br>
<br>

Network gets a marketplace for Journeys. These are hosted experiences with a cover photo, overview, description, duration, location, start dates, a price and a payment link. You can create, publish and manage your own journeys, buy one, and leave a review afterwards. The home feed becomes a Journal, and journeys, the Network feed, profiles and posts can now be viewed without logging in. The invite-code requirement is removed from the code.

<b>Key features</b>
<br>

- Host a Journey: title, overview, description, duration, location, start dates, price, cover photo, public or private visibility
- Journey detail pages with a purchase step through the journey's payment link
- My Journeys page (hosting and purchased), with active and archived lists
- Reviews after a journey
- Journal tab added to the navigation
- Public pages for logged-out visitors and a separate sign-in screen
- New "Journeys" section in the Terms
