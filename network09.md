Network v0.9: Artifacts & Sharing



October 2026



v0.9 is about how artifacts are found and shared. The Network feed gets an artifacts view with filters, artifact links get proper share previews with their covers, and the feeling box gets a small polish. Together with the v0.8 feeling box, this is the version where an artifact is no longer just a page you open — it is something you can find in the feed and share with its cover attached.

Key features



- Network feed artifacts and filters: All, Posts, and Artifacts
- Artifact share-link previews (Open Graph and Twitter) with artifact covers
- Network logo as the default share image
- Home avatar vertically centred with the feeling box
