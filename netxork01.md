<b><font size="5">Netxork v0.1: Fragments, Feed & Nodes</font></b>
<br>
<br>
<small>January 2026</small>
<br>
<br>

This is the first Network app. You write short private "fragments" in your own space (the Nexus) and can publish them to a public Network feed. A published fragment is permanent, but you can withdraw it. Private one-to-one conversations, called Nodes, start from a fragment and lock as read-only 42 hours after the first message. Sign-up uses an invite code, and accounts have email verification and two-factor authentication.

<b>Key features</b>
<br>

- Private fragments in the Nexus, published to a public Network feed
- Withdraw a published post, which redacts it and unlocks the fragment for editing again
- Starred posts and post detail pages
- Nodes: private 1:1 conversations that lock after 42 hours
- Invite-code sign-up, email verification, 2FA with an authenticator app (QR code and manual key)
- Account settings (display name, email, password) and an avatar upload through Cloudinary
- Built-in Terms of Service and Privacy Policy pages
