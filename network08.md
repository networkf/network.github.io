Network v0.8: Feeling Box & Mind Tools




October 2026




v0.8 is the baseline after v0.7 was archived into its own repo. The build clears 326 TypeScript errors with no behavior change, then fixes seven reachable runtime bugs in their own pull request. From there the product adds a feeling box, short mind-tool artifacts matched to how someone feels, a clearer journal path, and polish on galleries and duration.

The homepage composer puts an avatar on the left and a round up-arrow on the right. You describe your state; matching stays in the browser and returns published practices (about five minutes) whose descriptions do not open with “About 5 min” — duration is its own line between state and category. Gallery photo URLs are optional. The front-page plus opens the journal; Create artifact sits under My Artifacts. A Brooklyn Navy Yard pitch page is also in the set.

Key features



- Feeling box (avatar on the left, up-arrow explore)
- Mind-tool artifacts matched to mental state, with state, duration, and optional gallery_urls
- Cover required to post; Save Draft still allowed without one
- Favorites on the artifact page; Explore / Favorites filter
- Journal from the front-page plus; Create under My Artifacts
- Type-clean build and seven runtime bug fixes from the v0.7 baseline
- /brooklynnavyyardpitch page
